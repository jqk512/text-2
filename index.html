航空航天 · 生成式 AI

本次图 4、5、6（题目及答案） · 17 句
  1. According to Paragraph 1, the aerospace industry is known for its precision and exacting standards, and generative AI is revolutionizing its approach to design, production, maintenance, and mission planning.

    根据第1段，航空航天工业以精密性和严格的标准著称，生成式人工智能正在革新其设计、生产、维护和任务规划方式。

  2. Paragraph 2 states that AI encompasses technologies from data analysis to machine learning (ML) models, which allow systems to analyze data, recognize patterns, and make predictions.

    第2段指出，人工智能涵盖从数据分析到机器学习模型的技术，使系统能够分析数据、识别模式并作出预测。

  3. According to Paragraph 3, generative AI is a subset of AI that utilizes algorithms like GANs to create new data, such as designs or configurations.

    根据第3段，生成式人工智能是人工智能的一个子集，它利用生成对抗网络等算法创造新的数据，例如设计方案或配置。

  4. Paragraph 4 highlights the distinction: AI is used for maintenance and diagnostics, while generative AI enables breakthrough designs that align with goals like fuel efficiency or aerodynamics.

    第4段强调了两者的区别：人工智能用于维护和故障诊断，而生成式人工智能能创造突破性设计，以实现燃油效率或空气动力学方面的目标。

  5. In Paragraph 5, generative AI redefines aircraft design by focusing on goals such as weight reduction, fuel efficiency, and structural strength.

    第5段指出，生成式人工智能通过聚焦减重、燃油效率和结构强度等目标，重新定义飞机设计。

  6. Paragraph 6 gives an example where generative AI can produce lattice structures for interior components, which helps companies like Airbus and Boeing streamline design cycles.

    第6段举例说明，生成式人工智能能够为内部部件设计点阵结构，帮助空客和波音等公司精简设计周期。

  7. According to Paragraph 7, generative AI transforms maintenance by allowing for data-driven maintenance strategies based on historical data and real-time sensor information.

    根据第7段，生成式人工智能可依据历史数据和实时传感器信息制定数据驱动的维护策略，从而改变维护方式。

  8. Paragraph 8 states that by monitoring critical components like engines and landing gear, predictive maintenance can prevent mechanical issues, leading to more reliable flight operations.

    第8段指出，通过监测发动机和起落架等关键部件，预测性维护能预防机械故障，使飞行运行更加可靠。

  9. In Paragraph 9, a top priority is fuel efficiency, and generative AI helps engineers create aircraft that optimize airflow, reduce drag, and maximize fuel efficiency by enabling rapid prototyping.

    第9段指出，燃油效率是首要任务之一；生成式人工智能通过支持快速原型制造，帮助工程师设计出优化气流、减小阻力并最大限度提高燃油效率的飞机。

  10. Paragraph 10 mentions innovations like advanced winglets and blended wing body designs, which contribute to a reduction in operational costs and environmental impact.

    第10段提到先进翼梢小翼和翼身融合设计等创新，这些创新有助于降低运营成本和环境影响。

  11. According to Paragraph 11, autonomy in aerospace encompasses drones and navigation systems, and generative AI advances autonomous navigation in complex environments.

    根据第11段，航空航天领域的自主性涵盖无人机和导航系统，而生成式人工智能推动了复杂环境中的自主导航。

  12. Paragraph 12 explains that generative AI allows autonomous systems to learn through simulated flight hours, enabling real-time adjustments and enhancing autopilot systems and route optimization.

    第12段解释说，生成式人工智能让自主系统通过模拟飞行时数进行学习，从而能够实时调整，并改进自动驾驶系统和航线优化。

  13. In Paragraph 13, the aerospace industry relies on an intricate supply chain, and generative AI offers predictive solutions to manage inventory and forecast demand.

    第13段指出，航空航天工业依赖复杂的供应链，生成式人工智能提供预测性解决方案，以管理库存并预测需求。

  14. Paragraph 14 emphasizes that a streamlined supply chain minimizes costs and allows companies to adapt to changes in demand or unexpected disruptions.

    第14段强调，精简的供应链能降低成本，使企业适应需求变化或意外中断。

  15. According to Paragraph 15, generative AI is also transforming the passenger experience by creating efficient and customized cabin interiors.

    根据第15段，生成式人工智能还通过设计高效且定制化的客舱内部空间，改变乘客体验。

  16. Paragraph 16 states that generative AI allows airlines to experiment with different cabin configurations, balancing comfort, space, and operational efficiency.

    第16段指出，生成式人工智能使航空公司能够尝试不同的客舱配置，兼顾舒适度、空间和运营效率。

  17. Finally, Paragraph 17 concludes that generative AI is providing a robust tool for innovation and efficiency, paving the way for faster development cycles and a more sustainable future.

    最后，第17段总结说，生成式人工智能为创新和效率提升提供了有力工具，为更快的开发周期和更可持续的未来铺平道路。

智能制造 · 人工智能

本次图 1、2、3（题目及答案） · 18 句
  1. According to Paragraph 1, the fully autonomous factory is described as a provocative vision often seen in speculative fiction, but this approach is unlikely to be how AI is used in manufacturing in the near future.

    根据第1段，完全自主运行的工厂被描述为一种常见于科幻作品、引人遐想的愿景，但在不久的将来，人工智能不太可能以这种方式应用于制造业。

  2. Paragraph 2 defines the realistic conception of AI in manufacturing as machines that can operate autonomously and respond to events in intelligent, humanlike ways.

    第2段将制造业中较现实的人工智能定义为能够自主运行，并以智能、类人的方式应对事件的机器。

  3. According to Paragraph 3, AI in manufacturing involves machines performing humanlike tasks, such as detecting a tool wearing out and working around the problem.

    根据第3段，制造业中的人工智能使机器执行类似人类的任务，例如察觉工具正在磨损并设法绕过这一问题。

  4. Paragraph 4 places the current era of human enhancement through computers within the broader context of the Information Age or the Silicon Age.

    第4段将当今计算机增强人类能力的时代置于更广阔的信息时代或硅时代背景中。

  5. Paragraph 5 states that as computers take on traditional human tasks, AI becomes a natural development that can help creative people do more, rather than necessarily replace them.

    第5段指出，随着计算机承担传统的人类任务，人工智能自然发展为一种帮助有创造力的人做更多事情的工具，而不一定要取代他们。

  6. Paragraph 6 discusses the evolution towards safer and more efficient collaboration between humans and robots as AI makes the latter smarter.

    第6段讨论了随着人工智能使机器人变得更聪明，人和机器人之间逐渐形成更安全、更高效的协作。

  7. Paragraph 7 suggests that AI, combined with VR and AR, can optimize processes by improving the speed and precision of line workers, and will be applied to areas like process improvement and optimizing energy consumption.

    第7段认为，人工智能结合虚拟现实和增强现实，可以提高生产线工人的速度和精度，从而优化流程，并可用于流程改进和能源消耗优化等领域。

  8. Paragraph 8 envisions an ideal situation where smart, integrated systems with sensors allow personnel to focus on innovation instead of repetitive work.

    第8段设想了这样一种理想情形：配有传感器的智能集成系统使人员能够专注于创新，而非重复性工作。

  9. Paragraph 9 identifies expensive and scarce in-house capabilities as key sources of resistance to AI adoption among manufacturers.

    第9段指出，昂贵而稀缺的内部技术能力是制造商采用人工智能时遭遇阻力的主要原因。

  10. Paragraph 10 proposes that “factory in a box” and digital twin capabilities make adopting new processes less risky, especially for SMEs.

    第10段提出，“盒中工厂”和数字孪生能力可以降低采用新流程的风险，尤其对中小企业如此。

  11. Paragraph 11 describes predictive maintenance as a key AI application, using pretrained models that incorporate a tool’s cumulative knowledge to prevent problems.

    第11段将预测性维护描述为人工智能的一项关键应用：它使用包含工具累积知识的预训练模型来预防问题。

  12. Paragraph 12 states that quality inspection is naturally suited to machine learning because it generates vast amounts of data, a volume beyond human scope for analysis.

    第12段指出，质量检测天然适合机器学习，因为它会产生海量数据，数据量超出了人类的分析能力。

  13. Paragraph 13 explains that AR glasses can visualize the assembly process and offer prompts to workers, such as indicating when a task is complete.

    第13段解释说，增强现实眼镜可以将装配过程可视化，并向工人提供提示，例如提示某项任务何时完成。

  14. Paragraph 14 notes a difference in focus areas between larger companies and small medium enterprises(SMEs), with the latter tending to make a lot of parts.

    第14段指出，大型公司与中小企业的关注领域不同，后者往往生产大量零部件。

  15. Paragraph 15 introduces smart components as an emerging trend, which are parts with embedded sensors that monitor their own condition and stress.

    第15段介绍了智能部件这一新趋势：这类部件内置传感器，能够监测自身的状态和受力情况。

  16. Paragraph 16 gives an example of a company using cold metal fusion additive technology to embed sensors into machines.

    第16段举例介绍一家公司利用冷金属熔融增材技术将传感器嵌入机器。

  17. Paragraph 17 states that a smart component can cut down on data traffic by only reporting its normal status until conditions go sideways.

    第17段指出，智能部件在情况出现异常前只报告正常状态，从而减少数据传输量。

  18. Paragraph 18 concludes that the most immediate opportunity for AI to add value is in additive manufacturing, due to its expensive, low-volume products.

    第18段总结说，增材制造的产品昂贵且产量较低，因此它是人工智能近期最有机会创造价值的领域。
